# Self by subtraction — a lesion/dropout program

## Status

This is a research program, not a theory of consciousness and not a localization claim.

The motivating observation is simple: a large cognitive system can lose a major capacity while the person still reports that they remain present. Language, vision, memory, metacognition, motor output and bodily-self processing are therefore poor candidates for being equated one-to-one with the subject.

The useful question is not initially:

> Where is the self?

It is:

> **Which capacities can be removed while experience / selfhood / agency survive, and which failures actually abolish them?**

That turns neurology into an ablation experiment.

---

## 1. Do not collapse four different targets

The literature repeatedly punishes any attempt to use one behavioral variable as a synonym for consciousness.

Keep at least these separate:

```text
CONSCIOUS STATE
    is there wakeful / aware experience at all?

CONSCIOUS CONTENT
    what is experienced?

SELF / SELF-AWARENESS
    self-location, self-recognition, ownership, agency, autobiographical self,
    metacognitive self-knowledge, etc.

CONTROL / AGENCY
    which currently represented states can actually alter what the organism does next?

REPORT
    can the organism express any of the above through language or movement?
```

A lesion or transient dysfunction can damage one of these while sparing another.

This matters immediately for `PresentMoment`: **current causal leverage is not identical to consciousness, and consciousness is not identical to report.**

---

## 2. Language is an interface, not an acceptable location for the subject

Aphasia can selectively damage comprehension, naming, repetition, speech production, syntax or semantic processing depending on the affected network.

Clinical work on disorders of consciousness explicitly warns that receptive aphasia can make residual consciousness harder to detect behaviorally.

The strongest modern dissociation is cognitive-motor dissociation: Bodien et al. (NEJM, 2024; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2400645) found task-based fMRI/EEG command-following in 60/241 (~25%) patients who showed no observable command-following at bedside.

That does not mean all behaviorally unresponsive patients are conscious. It means:

```text
no useful outward report
    !=
no internal task following
```

Likewise, language failure cannot be treated as disappearance of the subject.

For epilepsy specifically, ictal/post-ictal language disturbances can include expressive, receptive or global aphasia. A 2026 video-EEG study (Ferri et al., Brain and Language 277:105766; DOI 10.1016/j.bandl.2026.105766) found post-ictal aphasia in a substantial subset of patients with adequately assessed focal seizures, with temporal-lobe and spread-related associations. This is relevant as a clinical dissociation, not as evidence for a consciousness mechanism.

---

## 3. Large pieces of the canonical "self network" can disappear without abolishing self-awareness

Philippi et al. (PLOS ONE, 2012; DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0038413) studied a rare patient with extensive bilateral damage involving insula, anterior cingulate, medial prefrontal cortex and medial temporal structures.

Despite severe amnesia and impairment of autobiographical self, the patient retained substantial core self-recognition, agency, stable self-concept and introspective self-awareness.

One extraordinary case cannot establish a distributed theory of self.

But it is a strong falsifier of a simple claim such as:

```text
insula = self
ACC = self
mPFC = self
medial temporal memory = self
```

The lesson is methodological:

> **candidate "self regions" should be tested by necessity, not by activation maps.**

---

## 4. Even metacognition is separable

Fleming et al. (Brain, 2014; DOI 10.1093/brain/awu221) found that anterior-prefrontal lesions selectively impaired perceptual metacognitive accuracy while objective perceptual performance remained comparable to controls; memory metacognition was relatively preserved.

So the ability to know how well one is seeing can be degraded without equivalently degrading seeing itself.

This adds another subtraction:

```text
experience / task performance
    !=
confidence calibration about that experience
```

A Pivot-like biological control surface cannot simply be identified with introspection either.

---

## 5. Bodily self variables are also dissociable

Insular / temporoparietal lesions and stimulation can alter interoception, self-location, ownership and autoscopic phenomena without simply turning the subject off.

For example, Ronchi et al. (Neuropsychologia, 2015; DOI 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2015.02.010) reported reduced heartbeat awareness and altered cardio-visual bodily-self effects after right-insular resection.

This says something important for the heart/body branch of `PresentMoment`:

> body signals may strongly shape the current self-model without being identical to the existence of the subject.

---

## 6. The remaining candidate looks more like a maintained regime than a cortical point

Current causal evidence increasingly implicates thalamocortical and corticocortical interactions in maintaining conscious state.

Redinbaugh et al. (Neuron, 2020; DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.01.005) found that central-lateral thalamus and deep cortical layers tracked changes across wakefulness, sleep and anesthesia in macaques; appropriately targeted thalamic stimulation restored arousal and wake-like processing under anesthesia.

Recent human work also gives the thalamus a causal role in perceptual access. A 2025/2026 human low-intensity focused-ultrasound study reported that modulation of ventroanterior thalamus changed visual-recognition sensitivity (PMID 41315300).

None of this licenses:

```text
thalamus = consciousness
```

A better working picture is:

```text
brainstem / arousal systems
          ↕
     thalamic loops
          ↕
 distributed cortical contents
          ↕
 sensorimotor / body loops
```

The subject may depend on a **maintained recurrent regime** whose contents and interfaces can change dramatically while the regime survives.

---

## 7. This changes the meaning of a biological PivotPoint

The software `PivotPoint` asks:

> what can I do now that changes what I will be able to read next?

The brain-side analogue should NOT be "a tiny executive neuron choosing everything".

A more defensible object is:

```text
                         maintained conscious regime
                                   |
                   currently public / potent subspaces
                                   |
               +-------------------+-------------------+
               |                   |                   |
           language            memory              body state
            online?             online?              online?
               |                   |                   |
          action/query        action/query        action/query
               +-------------------+-------------------+
                                   |
                             next accessible state
```

The "pivot" is therefore not necessarily an anatomical point.

It may be the **current control-potent surface inside a larger recurrent conscious regime**.

A language system can temporarily fall out of that surface while auditory experience, emotion, body state and other control routes remain.

A motor system can fall out while covert task-following remains.

A memory system can fail while self-recognition survives.

That is much closer to the lived dissociations than "the self is the whole brain" or "the self is the frontal lobe".

---

## 8. Public/private subspaces become directly relevant

`PUBLIC_AND_PRIVATE_SUBSPACES.md` already distinguished:

```text
active state
    !=
decodable state
    !=
receiver-legible state
    !=
behaviorally potent state
```

The lesion literature adds a fifth question:

```text
if this subspace disappears,
does the subject disappear,
or merely one interface / content / control route?
```

This suggests that "I" should not be identified with all currently active state.

A great deal of the brain is private to the current control surface.

Conversely, some processes may remain conscious but temporarily lose outward potency, as cognitive-motor dissociation demonstrates.

So there are at least two orthogonal axes:

```text
conscious / not conscious

and

control-potent / control-null for this receiver/action
```

Do not collapse them.

---

## 9. The actual experiment: build a dropout matrix

Do not invent another scalar consciousness score.

Build a table from lesion, stimulation, sleep/anesthesia and epilepsy literature.

Each row is an externally observed perturbation:

```text
perturbation / lesion
anatomy or physiological manipulation
language
vision
memory
interoception
self-recognition
self-location
agency
metacognition
motor report
covert command-following
wakefulness
conscious content
recovery / compensation
```

The first objective is not machine learning.

It is to discover which columns are repeatedly dissociable.

Then ask whether any proposed "core" survives the ablations.

### Strong falsifier

If a proposed indispensable core structure/pathway can be bilaterally destroyed or functionally silenced while robust consciousness persists, remove it from the core.

### Opposite falsifier

If small perturbations to a proposed peripheral system repeatedly abolish consciousness across modalities while leaving basic arousal machinery intact, the peripheral label was wrong.

### Critical control

Separate inability to REPORT from inability to EXPERIENCE. Cognitive-motor dissociation makes this mandatory.

---

## 10. Do not over-read one person's symptom

A person's recurrent transient loss of language comprehension can motivate the question because it makes the modularity viscerally obvious.

It cannot by itself identify the failed circuit or prove that "the self" is elsewhere.

In epilepsy and after temporal surgery, transient language dysfunction has several possible mechanisms and requires clinical interpretation. Sudden new or atypically prolonged language loss also has non-epileptic emergency differentials such as stroke.

Use the subjective observation as a hypothesis generator only.

---

## 11. Current working sentence

The strongest version worth carrying forward is not:

> consciousness is a PivotPoint.

It is:

> **The contents and interfaces of a conscious organism can fail selectively. A useful brain-side PivotPoint, if such an object exists, is therefore more likely to be a dynamically maintained control-potent surface inside a distributed recurrent regime than a single cognitive module or anatomical location.**

That statement can still die.

The dropout matrix is how it earns the right to survive.
